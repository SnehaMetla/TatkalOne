IRCTC TATKAL BOOKING — UX PROBLEM & PROPOSED SOLUTION
=========================================================

PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------
IRCTC Tatkal ticket booking users feel lost and confused during the payment
process, especially when payment is deducted without ticket issuance or clear
refund timelines. This results in financial anxiety, wasted time, loss of trust,
unclear system feedback, and frustration caused by performance instability.

PROBLEM CONTEXT
---------------
Tatkal booking is a time-sensitive experience where users need to complete the
booking quickly. During the payment stage, uncertainty becomes a major issue
when the system does not clearly communicate whether the payment was
successful, whether the ticket has been issued, or what the user should do
next.

When money is deducted but a ticket is not issued, users may not know whether
to wait, retry the payment, or start the booking process again. The lack of
clear refund information adds further uncertainty and reduces confidence in
the system.

KEY USER PAIN POINTS
--------------------
- Payment deducted but ticket not issued
- Unclear payment success, failure, or pending status
- Unclear refund status and refund timelines
- Users do not know whether to wait or retry
- Poor or delayed system feedback
- Performance instability during the booking process
- Wasted time when users have to repeat steps
- Financial anxiety when money has been deducted
- Reduced trust in the booking system

USER IMPACT
-----------
The problem affects users beyond the payment screen. Unclear feedback can make
users repeatedly check their booking or bank account, retry transactions, or
restart the booking process. This increases stress and wastes valuable time
during the limited Tatkal booking window.

CORE INSIGHT
------------
The main issue is uncertainty.

Users need to know what is happening with their money and ticket at every
critical point in the payment journey. When the system does not provide clear
and timely feedback, users lose their sense of control and confidence.

DESIGN OPPORTUNITY
------------------
How might we make the IRCTC Tatkal payment and booking experience clearer and
more reliable, so users always understand their payment status, ticket status,
and refund timeline?

GOAL
----
Create a payment and booking experience that gives Tatkal users clarity,
control, and confidence, particularly when a transaction does not immediately
result in ticket confirmation.


PROPOSED SOLUTION
=================

To address this uncertainty, we introduced a visual booking status timeline at
the top of the screen.

The timeline brings the four most important stages of the booking journey
together in one place:

Payment → Booking Status → Ticket Status → Seat Allotment

Instead of making users interpret separate messages or guess what happened,
the timeline gives them a clear visual representation of where their booking
currently stands.

The timeline changes according to the user's booking situation.


FOUR-STAGE STATUS TIMELINE
--------------------------

1. PAYMENT
Shows whether the payment was:
- Payment Successful
- Payment Unsuccessful
- Payment Not Received

2. BOOKING STATUS
Shows whether the booking was successfully processed:
- Booking Successful
- Booking Unsuccessful

3. TICKET STATUS
Shows the current ticket status:
- Confirmed
- Waiting List
- Pending

4. SEAT ALLOTMENT
Shows the final seat outcome:
- Confirmed → Seat number
- RAC → RAC number
- Waiting List → Waiting-list number
- Failed / Pending → Corresponding status

This separation helps users understand that payment status, booking status,
ticket status, and seat status are not necessarily the same thing.


STATUS COLOUR SYSTEM
--------------------

GREEN — SUCCESSFUL / CONFIRMED
Used for:
- Payment successful
- Booking successful
- Ticket confirmed
- Confirmed seat allotted

YELLOW — RAC
Used when:
- RAC seat is allotted

ORANGE — WAITING LIST
Used when:
- Ticket is in the waiting-list state

RED — FAILED / PENDING
Used for:
- Payment unsuccessful
- Booking unsuccessful
- Ticket pending
- Seat allotment pending
- Seat allotment failed

A tick mark (✓) or cross mark (×) is placed inside the coloured timeline
circles to provide an additional visual cue.

This allows users to understand the status through both colour and icon,
rather than relying only on text.


SIX MAIN BOOKING SCENARIOS
==========================

SCENARIO 01 — FULLY CONFIRMED
Payment: Successful ✓
Booking: Successful ✓
Ticket: Confirmed
Seat: Confirmed seat allotted

The timeline shows a successful journey from payment to seat allotment.


SCENARIO 02 — RAC SEAT
Payment: Successful ✓
Booking: Successful ✓
Ticket: Confirmed
Seat: RAC seat allotted

The user can immediately understand that the ticket is confirmed but the seat
is RAC.


SCENARIO 03 — WAITING LIST + CONFIRMED SEAT
Payment: Successful ✓
Booking: Successful ✓
Ticket: Waiting List
Seat: Confirmed seat allotted

The timeline communicates the waiting-list ticket status while separately
showing the seat information.


SCENARIO 04 — WAITING LIST + RAC
Payment: Successful ✓
Booking: Successful ✓
Ticket: Waiting List
Seat: RAC seat allotted

The user can see both the waiting-list status and RAC information clearly.


SCENARIO 05 — WAITING LIST + SEAT ALLOTMENT FAILED
Payment: Successful ✓
Booking: Successful ✓
Ticket: Waiting List
Seat: Seat allotment failed

The timeline makes it clear that the payment and booking were successful, but
the issue occurred during seat allotment.


SCENARIO 06 — PAYMENT UNSUCCESSFUL
Payment: Unsuccessful ×
Booking: Unsuccessful ×
Ticket: Pending
Seat: Pending

The timeline clearly shows that the issue occurred at the payment/booking
stage while the later stages remain pending.


SUPPORTING BOOKING INFORMATION
------------------------------

Below the timeline, we provide a simple information card containing the
essential booking details:

- Train name / train details
- From → To station
- Passenger name / account-holder name

This gives users immediate context about which journey and passenger the status
belongs to.


DETAILED STATUS INFORMATION
---------------------------

The timeline provides the quick overview, while the section below provides
the exact status.

PAYMENT STATUS
Payment Successful / Payment Unsuccessful / Payment Not Received

BOOKING STATUS
Booking Successful / Booking Unsuccessful

TICKET STATUS
Confirmed / Waiting List / Pending

SEAT ALLOTMENT
- Confirmed → Seat number
- RAC → RAC number
- Waiting List → Waiting-list number
- Failed / Pending → Corresponding status


HOW THE SOLUTION ADDRESSES THE PROBLEM
--------------------------------------

The core problem is that users are uncertain about what happened after they
attempt to make a Tatkal booking.

Our solution answers four critical questions in a clear sequence:

Did my payment go through?
↓
Was my booking processed?
↓
Is my ticket confirmed or waitlisted?
↓
What seat/status did I receive?

The user no longer needs to interpret multiple messages or repeatedly check
whether the booking was successful.

The four-stage timeline makes the complete status visible in one place.


KEY UX BENEFIT
--------------

FROM UNCERTAINTY → TO CLARITY

The solution gives users a single, easy-to-understand view of their booking
status, helping them quickly identify:

- What has succeeded
- What has failed
- What is still pending
- What ticket status they received
- What seat status they received

Ultimately, the timeline aims to give Tatkal users more clarity and control
at the moment when uncertainty is highest.
